## §1 ID Convention & Scope

Operational Objectives carry IDs `PO-D-XX.Y-NNN` (personal-data ambit, GDPR) and `SO-D-XX.Y-NNN` (product/entity/AI ambits — CRA, NIS 2, DORA, AI Act). One row per applicable (sub-domain × regulation) pair; the statement is inherited verbatim from the modular baseline Sub-SO for that pair, and the Parent Goal points to the Phase 1 Adjusted Goal. Verification criteria/methods follow the regulation's evidence regime; anchors are the published CSF 2.0 sets of the cited baseline module (PF accompanies GDPR rows and AI RMF accompanies AI Act rows via the mapping store at Phase 3).
