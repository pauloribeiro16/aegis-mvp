# D-10.2 — Pair analysis: GDPR ↔ AI Act

> Cross-regulation layer: consult only when BOTH regulations are applicable.
> Canonical source: merged Part 1 §1 (deep) + Part 2 (summary).

##### Pair: GDPR ↔ AI_Act

**GDPR article (verbatim, Art. 5(2) + Art. 30(3)):**
> [as quoted above]

**AI_Act article (verbatim, Art. 12(1)+(2) + Art. 19(1)):**
> "High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the automatic recording of events (logs) over the lifetime of the system." — Art. 12(1) AI Act (per SR-AIACT-007)
> "Logging capabilities shall ensure a level of traceability of the functioning of a high-risk AI system that is appropriate to the intended purpose of the system, by enabling the recording of events relevant for: (a) ensuring traceability of the functioning of the system; (b) monitoring its operation; (c) facilitating post-market monitoring pursuant to Article 72." — Art. 12(2) AI Act
> "Providers of high-risk AI systems shall keep the logs referred to in Article 12(1), automatically generated by their high-risk AI systems and to the extent that such logs are under their control, for a period appropriate to the intended purpose of the high-risk AI system, and of at least six months, unless provided otherwise in the applicable Union or national law." — Art. 19(1) AI Act (per SR-AIACT-008/009; the T5 mapping flag from AIA-C11 to Art. 12(4) — which does not exist in the OJ — is preserved per the SR-AIACT-008 v0.2 audit)

**Scope comparison:**
- GDPR scope: compliance records with integrity + traceability.
- AI Act scope: automatic event logging with traceability + 6-month minimum retention + provider-side retention responsibility.
- Scope overlap: **Conditional (Y when the provider/deployer is a controller processing personal data)**.

**Trigger comparison:**
- GDPR trigger: processing of personal data.
- AI Act trigger: high-risk AI system lifetime automatic logging.
- Trigger alignment: **PARTIAL**.

**Threshold/timeline comparison:**
- GDPR: until processing ceases + on SA request.
- AI Act: ≥6-month minimum floor + `appropriate to the intended purpose`.
- Tension: **NONE — AI Act 6-month floor is a subset of GDPR's "as long as necessary" envelope.**

**Recipient comparison:**
- GDPR: data subject, DPA.
- AI Act: provider, MSA.

**Content template comparison:**
- GDPR: Art. 30(1)/(2) content items.
- AI Act: Art. 12(2) (a)–(c) traceability/monitoring/post-market monitoring.

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** COMPLEMENTARY
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **SAME — COMPLEMENTARY** (with floor-within-ceiling pattern)
**Reasoning:** GDPR compliance records + AI Act event logs (6-month retention) stack for AI systems processing personal data. The two regimes address different record types (compliance vs. AI-system events). **The AI Act 6-month floor is a subset of GDPR's "as long as necessary" envelope** — when the intended purpose requires 6+ months of logs, GDPR's "necessary" is satisfied for that period.

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **Conditional (Y when the high-risk AI system processes personal data and logs contain personal data)**. No reclassification required.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation must codify AI Act Art. 19(1) 6-month floor as a hard minimum within the GDPR Art. 5(1)(e) ceiling envelope. The two regimes are reconcilable within the intersection [6 months, "necessary"].
