### D-10.1 — Continuous Security Monitoring

> **Framing.** TinyTask Lda. is an 8-employee B2B SaaS productivity company (Portugal, EU) developing and operating a rule-based task-management platform (web + mobile interface) on managed cloud hosting. Regulatory roles: **controller** for customer account metadata (names, emails, passwords) and **processor** for customer task data containing end-user personal data; **manufacturer** of products with digital elements (SaaS + web/mobile interface) placed on the EU market in the default class with self-assessment conformity. No AI/ML systems are developed, deployed, or integrated. NIS 2, DORA, and AI Act are excluded per the fact sheet (non-critical service, <50 employees, non-financial, zero AI). The layered 5-regulation monitoring architecture in the corpus baseline is therefore narrowed to the two confirmed regulations (GDPR + CRA); the entity-layer mandatory monitoring (GDPR) and the product-layer monitoring with user opt-out (CRA) stack on the same SaaS platform.
