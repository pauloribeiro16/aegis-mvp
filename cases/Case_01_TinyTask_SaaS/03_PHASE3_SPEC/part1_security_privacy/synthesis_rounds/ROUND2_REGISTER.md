**Convergence:** Round 2 converged 2026-10-10 with 4/4 verdicts and no rejection. P1–P3 are MUST-level wording changes applied to the v2 baseline; P4(a) is a carve-out that routes one leg to the organizational lane. The accepted baseline is unchanged in substance — no constraint was removed, and one obligation that previously had no carrier now has a recorded route.

**Residual risk accepted by P4(a):** corruption or unauthorised modification of stored billing or subscription state is detected by the product but not surfaced by it. At 8-person scale the detection-to-human path is an operational monitoring duty, recorded in the organizational lane rather than as a product constraint. If the organization later adopts automated alerting on this class, P4(b) becomes the specification of record.
