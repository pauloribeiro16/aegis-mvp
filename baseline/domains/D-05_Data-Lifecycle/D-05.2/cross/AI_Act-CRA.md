# D-05.2 — Pair analysis: CRA ↔ AI Act

> Cross-regulation layer: consult only when BOTH regulations are applicable.
> Canonical source: merged Part 1 §1 (deep) + Part 2 (summary).

##### Pair: CRA ↔ AI_Act

**CRA article (verbatim, Annex I Part II (7)/(8)):**
> "Securely distribute updates … to the users of products with digital elements, in a timely manner, including, where appropriate, through automatic updates." — CRA Annex I Part II (7)
>
> "Ensure that updates are disseminated free of charge, are clearly described, contain information on the security improvements and on the actions the user should take." — CRA Annex I Part II (8)

**AI_Act article (verbatim, Art. 19(1)):**
> "Providers of high-risk AI systems shall retain the logs referred to in Article 12(1), automatically generated by the system, for a period appropriate to the intended purpose of the high-risk AI system, and of at least six months, unless Union or national law provides otherwise." — AI Act Art. 19(1)

**Scope comparison:**
- CRA scope: update artefact availability (manufacturer obligation).
- AI Act scope: AI-system log retention (provider obligation).
- Scope overlap: **N** — different objects (update artefacts vs AI-system logs), different actors (manufacturer vs provider).

**Trigger comparison:**
- CRA trigger: ongoing product support period.
- AI Act trigger: AI-system log generation by a high-risk AI system.
- Trigger alignment: **DIFFERENT**.

**Threshold/timeline comparison:**
- CRA: ≥5 years (Art. 13(8) — support period minimum); Art. 13(18) accessibility for at least 10 years after placing on the market for Annex II information. CRDA's "10-year tail" reading is interpretive but substantively defensible.
- AI Act: ≥6 months.
- Tension: **NONE — scope-disjoint.**

**Recipient comparison:**
- CRA: users + market surveillance authority.
- AI Act: market surveillance authority + national competent authority.

**Content template comparison:**
- CRA: not codified for content; Annex II §9 optional user access.
- AI Act: not codified for content; Art. 12(1) automatic event logging specifications.

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** DIFFERENT-PERSPECTIVE
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **SAME — DIFFERENT-PERSPECTIVE**
**Reasoning:** Different objects (update artefact availability vs AI log retention), different actors (manufacturer vs provider), different purposes. Complementary, not overlapping. CRDA's label is correct.

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **N** — distinct actors, distinct objects, distinct triggers.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation is straightforward: two parallel obligations. No layering required; both apply simultaneously when the same actor is both manufacturer (CRA) and provider (AI Act).
