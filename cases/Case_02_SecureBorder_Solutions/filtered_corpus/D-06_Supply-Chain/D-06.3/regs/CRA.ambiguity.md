## CRA
#### 3.10 CRA-CL24 — Art. 13(6) sentence 1 — Component-vulnerability handling
**Source locus:**
> Manufacturers shall, upon identifying a vulnerability in a component, including in an open source-component, which is integrated in the product with digital elements report the vulnerability to the person or entity manufacturing or maintaining the component, and **address and remediate** the vulnerability in accordance with the vulnerability handling requirements set out in Part II of Annex I.
**Instance 1 — COORD — S2 — `address AND remediate`**
Two distinct actions.
- **R1:** Both (address + remediate).
- **R2:** Either (synonymous).
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Both | In-Directive: literal `and` |
| R2 | Either | None in OJ |
R1 is the literal reading. S2.
*Berry anchor:* §5.4.7 (AND).
**Instance 2 — POLY — S2 — `address`**
- **R1:** Patch.
- **R2:** Workaround.
- **R3:** Mitigation (any).
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Patch | None in OJ |
| R2 | Workaround | None in OJ |
| R3 | Any | In-Directive: literal `address` |
R3 is the literal reading. S2.
*Berry anchor:* §3.3.1 (`address`).

#### 2.3 CRA-CL92 — Art. 20(1) — Distributor due care
**Source locus:**
> When making a product with digital elements available on the market, distributors shall act with **due care** in relation to the requirements set out in this Regulation.
**Instance 1 — VAG — S3 — `due care`**
Same `due care`/`due diligence` pattern as Art. 13(5) CL23a.
- **R1:** Procedural (documented verification).
- **R2:** Substantive (positive verification of compliance).
- **R3:** Both.
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Procedural | None in OJ |
| R2 | Substantive | In-Directive: literal `act with due care` |
| R3 | Both | In-Directive: literal |
R3 is the literal reading. S3.
*Berry anchor:* §5.1 (`due care`).
