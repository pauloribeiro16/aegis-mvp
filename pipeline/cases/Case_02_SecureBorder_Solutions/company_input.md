# Company Input — SecureBorder Solutions B.V.

## §1 Company Profile

SecureBorder Solutions B.V. is a private limited company (B.V.) registered and
headquartered in the Netherlands, with its operations in Amsterdam. It operates
in the defence, security and critical infrastructure sector, supplying
automated border-control gates and identity management to airport and border
operations across multiple Member States — a service the intake classifies as
essential/critical. It has 450 employees and approximately €120M annual revenue,
placing it in the EU Medium-Large size band. It is not a financial entity and
provides no ICT services to financial entities.

## §2 Product / Service

SecureBorder manufactures and operates automated border-control eGate kiosks —
hardware combined with on-board AI verification software — used at airports and
border crossings. It also supplies identity-management capability alongside the
gates, and operates its own 24/7 security operations centre covering per-airport
deployments. Border control officers are in the loop for every decision and can
review and override the AI output in real time.

## §3 Data Handled

The company processes traveller data on a continuous, large-scale basis:
passport data, watchlist-derived decisions, and traveller biometric templates
processed as special-category data under Art. 9. It is a processor for
traveller data (government authorities act as controllers) and a controller for
employee data and audit logs. Processing rests on the Art. 9(2)(g) substantial
public-interest basis rather than consent, so a DPIA is mandatory by statute and
raw biometric captures are discarded immediately after template extraction.
Protection goes beyond standard Art. 32 measures (encryption, access control,
audit trails, retention limits) because of the biometric sensitivity.

## §4 Systems & Cloud

Per-airport eGate deployments are operated by the company, with a documented and
annually tested business-continuity arrangement and a formal supply-chain
security programme (hardware suppliers audited, software bills of materials per
release, continuous component-vulnerability monitoring). A certified
information-security and privacy management system is audited annually. The
security organisation exceeds 25 staff with an appointed CISO and a mandatory
DPO. Security training runs annually with role-specific curricula, and a
dedicated internal audit function verifies compliance.

## §5 Regulations Assumed

- **GDPR** — continuous large-scale processing of special-category biometric and
  passport data, as a processor for traveller data and a controller for employee
  data.
- **CRA** — places products with digital elements (eGate kiosks: hardware plus
  on-board AI software) on the EU market as a manufacturer; the border-control
  security function makes these Critical Class products.
- **NIS2** — border control is an Annex I essential sector, and as a supplier of
  services to essential entities the Art. 21 risk-management and Art. 23
  incident-notification duties bind directly.
- **AI Act** — develops and operates an AI system for border-control management,
  an Annex III high-risk use case; acts as provider of the on-board verification
  AI and as deployer through airport and government customers.
- **DORA is assumed NOT to apply** — the company is neither a financial entity
  nor an ICT third-party service provider to financial entities, so both limbs of
  Art. 2 fail.
