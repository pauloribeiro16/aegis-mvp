##### Pair: GDPR ↔ DORA

> **Note:** CRDA did not explicitly analyze this pair for D-08.2 (CRDA lists 5 pairs only).

**GDPR article (verbatim, Art. 39(1)(b)):**
> [as quoted above]

**DORA article (verbatim, Art. 13(6)):**
> "Financial entities shall develop ICT security awareness programmes and digital operational resilience training as compulsory modules in their staff training schemes, with a level of complexity commensurate to the remit of their functions." — Art. 13(6) DORA (per SR-DORA-025 — `commensurate to the remit`)

**Scope comparison:**
- GDPR scope: DPO-driven role-specific training.
- DORA scope: compulsory ICT security awareness + digital operational resilience training (workforce, `commensurate to the remit`).
- Scope overlap: **Y (when the DORA financial entity is a controller processing personal data with role-specific staff).**

**Trigger comparison:**
- GDPR trigger: DPO-catalysed role-specific training.
- DORA trigger: compulsory-modules-in-training-schemes (entity-side).
- Trigger alignment: **PARTIAL** (both staff-targeted but different governance hooks).

**Threshold/timeline comparison:**
- GDPR: no quantitative cadence.
- DORA: `compulsory` + `commensurate to the remit` — entity-discretionary cadence, function-specific depth.
- Tension: **NONE**.

**Recipient comparison:**
- GDPR: data subject, DPA.
- DORA: management body, NCA.

**Content template comparison:**
- GDPR: DPO-discretionary curriculum.
- DORA: function-specific depth.

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** NOT CLASSIFIED (CRDA omitted this pair for D-08.2)
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **COMPLEMENTARY** (recommended)
**Reasoning:** DORA's `commensurate to the remit` clause subsumes GDPR's role-specific layer when the DORA financial entity is a controller. The two regimes stack with the DORA programme as the carrier and the GDPR role-specific module as a sub-component. The `commensurate to the remit` anchor is the broadest scope and absorbs the GDPR role-specific dimension in principle (per SR-DORA-025 reasoning).

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **Y (when the DORA financial entity is a controller with role-specific staff)**. No reclassification required.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation must produce a DORA-led stack: DORA Art. 13(6) `commensurate to the remit` carrier + GDPR Art. 39(1)(b) role-specific module. CRDA's omission should be corrected by adding a "complementary" classification.
