## CRA
#### 3.9 CRA-CL23a — Art. 13(5) — Supply-chain due diligence (the Q6=b split locus)
**Source locus:**
> For the purpose of complying with paragraph 1, manufacturers shall **exercise due diligence when integrating components sourced from third parties** so that those components do not compromise the cybersecurity of the product with digital elements, including when integrating components of free and open-source software that have not been made available on the market in the course of a commercial activity.
**Instance 1 — VAG — S3 — `due diligence`**
`due diligence` — case-law-laden term imported from company law (M&A context). Not defined in CRA. Three readings:
- **R1:** Procedural (documented vetting process).
- **R2:** Substantive (positive verification of supplier security posture).
- **R3:** Both.
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Procedural | None in OJ |
| R2 | Substantive | In-Directive: literal `so that those components do not compromise the cybersecurity` |
| R3 | Both | In-Directive: literal |
R3 is the literal reading. R1 vs R2 admit materially different compliance populations (R1 admits paper-based; R2 requires verification). S3.
*Berry anchor:* §5.1 (`due diligence`).
**Instance 2 — POLY — S2 — `components sourced from third parties`**
`third parties` — who counts?
- **R1:** External suppliers (commercial vendors).
- **R2:** OSS developers (non-commercial).
- **R3:** Both.
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Commercial | In-Directive: literal |
| R2 | OSS | In-Directive: literal `including when integrating components of free and open-source software` |
| R3 | Both | In-Directive: literal |
R3 is the literal reading. S2.
*Berry anchor:* §3.3.1 (`third parties`).
**Instance 3 — SCOPE-Q — S2 — `free and open-source software that have not been made available on the market in the course of a commercial activity`**
OSS scope limitation. The reading:
- **R1:** Non-commercial OSS only (the explicit carve-out).
- **R2:** All OSS (the carve-out is descriptive).
| # | Reading | Disambiguation source |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Non-commercial | In-Directive: literal `that have not been made available on the market in the course of a commercial activity` |
| R2 | All OSS | None in OJ |
R1 is the literal reading. S2.
*Berry anchor:* §3.3.1 (`commercial activity`); §5.1.
**Layer 1 observation** — T2's CRA-C24 mapped this clause as "10-year documentation retention" — the substance is actually Art. 13(13) (CL38). Per Q6=b, v0.2 splits the T2 mapping error into CL23a (Art. 13(5) due diligence) + CL38 (Art. 13(13) 10-year docs). The Art. 13(5) clause is itself the most operationally consequential supply-chain due-diligence provision in CRA, and inherits the unresolved `due diligence` Berry-classic.
