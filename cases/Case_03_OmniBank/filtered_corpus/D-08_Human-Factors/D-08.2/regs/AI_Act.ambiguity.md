## AI_Act
#### 2.14 AIA-C14 — Art. 14(1) Human oversight design
**Source locus:** `High-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way, including with appropriate human-machine interface tools, that they can be effectively overseen by natural persons during the period in which they are in use`.
* **VAG — S2** — `appropriate`, `effectively overseen`.
* **POLY — S2** — `effectively overseen` — supervisor-can-intervene vs. supervisor-does-intervene vs. supervisor-understands. The three senses drive different design choices (stop-button vs. dashboard vs. explanation-panel).
**Berry anchor:** §5.1 (`appropriate`, `effectively`), §3.3.1 (`overseen`).
#### 2.15 AIA-C15 — Art. 14(4) Oversight measures content
**Source locus:** `natural persons to whom human oversight is assigned are enabled, as appropriate and proportionate: (a) to properly understand … (b) to remain aware … (c) to correctly interpret … (d) to decide … (e) to intervene …`.
* **VAG — S2** — `as appropriate and proportionate` (soft hedge opening the entire list).
* **POLY — S2** — `automation bias` (named in (b)) — technical term imported from human-factors research without definition.
* **COORD — S2** — 5-element list (a)–(e) of oversight abilities AND-coordinated.
**Berry anchor:** §5.1 (`appropriate`, `proportionate`), §3.3.1 (`automation bias`), §5.4.7.
#### 2.24 AIA-C24 — Art. 26(2) Deployer oversight assignment
**Source locus:** `Deployers shall assign human oversight to natural persons who have the necessary competence, training and authority, as well as the necessary support`.
* **VAG — S2** — `necessary`.
* **POLY — S2** — `competence`, `training`, `authority` — three near-synonyms for personnel qualifications.
* **COORD — S2** — `necessary competence, training AND authority` (3-way AND); `as well as the necessary support` adds a fourth element.
**Berry anchor:** §5.1 (`necessary`), §3.3.1 (`competence`/`training`/`authority`), §5.4.7.
