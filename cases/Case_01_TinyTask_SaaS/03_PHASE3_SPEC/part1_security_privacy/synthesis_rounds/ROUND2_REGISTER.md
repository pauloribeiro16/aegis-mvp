# Round 2 Register — Coverage Re-Audit under Fulfillment Semantics (TinyTask SaaS)

> **Mandate:** approved working plan, 2026-09-28 ("Codificar regra + reparar Case_01") — re-audit the 26 "Covered" rows of [`OBJECTIVE_MAPPING.md`](OBJECTIVE_MAPPING.md) under the fulfillment semantics now codified in [ADR-007](../../../../../docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28_requirement-objective-fulfillment-semantics.md), apply record corrections, and table baseline-change proposals for the client.
