# Case 03 — OmniBank Financial Systems: Operational Objectives

> **Version:** 1.1.0 (2026-09-28) · **Upstream:** Phase 1 `Doc02_Adjusted_Goals.md` (134 AGs) · **Downstream:** Phase 3 consumes these objectives + the 7 principles (`Doc02_Secure_Design_Principles.md`).

## §1 ID Convention & Scope

Operational Objectives carry IDs `PO-D-XX.Y-NNN` (personal-data ambit, GDPR) and `SO-D-XX.Y-NNN` (product/entity/AI ambits — CRA, NIS 2, DORA, AI Act). One row per applicable (sub-domain × regulation) pair with a baseline Sub-SO; the statement is inherited verbatim from the modular baseline, and the Parent Goal points to the Phase 1 Adjusted Goal. Verification criteria/methods follow the regulation’s evidence regime; anchors are the published CSF 2.0 sets of the cited baseline module (PF accompanies GDPR rows and AI RMF accompanies AI Act rows via the mapping store at Phase 3).
