## AI_Act
#### 2.28 AIA-C28 — Art. 55(1)(a) GPAI model evaluation
**Source locus:** `perform model evaluation in accordance with standardised protocols and tools reflecting the state of the art, including conducting and documenting adversarial testing of the model with a view to identifying and mitigating systemic risks`.
* **VAG — S3** — `standardised protocols` (which standards? ISO/IEC? NIST? MLCommons?), `state of the art` (Berry classic, time-dependent), `systemic risks` (undefined at Union level for AI).
* **POLY — S2** — `adversarial testing` — distinct from red-teaming, fuzzing, and robustness testing; the term is borrowed from cybersecurity but applied to model evaluation.
* **COORD — S2** — `conducting AND documenting` (AND); `identifying AND mitigating` (AND).
**Berry anchor:** §5.1 (`standardised`, `state of the art`, `systemic`), §3.3.1 (`adversarial testing`).
