- CRA: "due care" + non-conformity escalation.
- DORA: closed 9-element list.
- Tension: **NONE**.

**Recipient comparison:**
- CRA: MSA + manufacturer.
- DORA: competent authority.

**Content template comparison:**
- CRA: pre-market verification + non-conformity escalation + corrective measures.
- DORA: 9-element Art. 30(2) contract.

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** DIFFERENT-PERSPECTIVE
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **SAME — DIFFERENT-PERSPECTIVE**
**Reasoning:** CRA economic-operator obligations are *market-surveillance-facing* (the obligation is to the MSA, not to a contracting party); DORA Art. 30 is *contractual* (the obligation is to the financial entity and the competent authority via the contract). Both apply when a CRA-classified product is procured by a financial entity.

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **N (typically) — distinct mechanisms, distinct contracting parties.** Conditional overlap when a CRA-classified product is procured by a financial entity.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation produces a 2-layer architecture: (1) CRA economic-operator obligations to MSA; (2) DORA ICT third-party contract with the financial entity. The DIFFERENT-PERSPECTIVE label is preserved.
