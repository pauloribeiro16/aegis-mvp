## AI_Act
#### 2.25 AIA-C25 — Art. 72(1)-(2) Post-market monitoring
**Source locus:** `Providers shall establish and document a post-market monitoring system in a manner that is proportionate to the nature of the AI technologies and the risks of the high-risk AI system. The post-market monitoring system shall actively and systematically collect, document and analyse relevant data`.
* **VAG — S2** — `proportionate to the nature`, `relevant data`.
* **POLY — S2** — `post-market monitoring system` — borrows from medical-device regulation (MDR Art. 83) but applied to AI; the borrow is implicit.
* **COORD — S2** — `actively AND systematically` (adverbial AND); `collect, document AND analyse` (3-way verb AND).
**Berry anchor:** §5.1 (`proportionate`, `relevant`), §3.3.1 (`post-market monitoring`), §5.5.
#### 2.27 AIA-C27 — Art. 74(1) Market surveillance
**Source locus:** `Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 shall apply to AI systems covered by this Regulation. For the purposes of the effective enforcement of this Regulation: (a) any reference to an economic operator … shall be understood as including all operators identified in Article 2(1) of this Regulation; (b) any reference to a product … shall be understood as including all AI systems falling within the scope of this Regulation`.
* **POLY — S2** — `market surveillance` — borrows the entire Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 framework, including its definitions, procedures, and authority powers. The borrow is implicit; operators unfamiliar with 2019/1020 cannot read this clause self-contained.
**Berry anchor:** §3.3.1 (`market surveillance`, cross-regulation borrow).
