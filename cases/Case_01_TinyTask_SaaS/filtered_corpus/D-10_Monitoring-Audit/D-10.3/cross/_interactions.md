## Cross-recipient map

#### Cross-recipient map
D-10.3 generates a layered recipient set on the **testing-evidence trail**. The recipients are at distinct regulatory channels on the testing-evidence object (test reports + effectiveness-assessment outputs + conformity-assessment certificates + TLPT reports + AI-system testing outputs). The channels feed distinct regulatory artefacts and do NOT generate a notification cascade of D-10.3 itself — the testing-evidence duty is upstream of any compliance audit and feeds the conformity-assessment, MSA inspection, supervisory review, and management-body oversight channels:

- **NIS 2** — management body + competent authority (CSIRT or NCA) on the Art. 21(2)(f) effectiveness-assessment outputs (policies and procedures to assess the effectiveness of cybersecurity risk-management measures, as part of the all-hazards risk-management framework).
- **CRA** — market surveillance authority (MSA) on the Annex VII §6 test reports (reports of tests carried out to verify conformity of the product with digital elements and of the vulnerability-handling processes with the Annex I Part I and Part II cybersecurity requirements) + Annex VIII Part I (Module A internal production control self-assessment) + Annex VIII Part II (Module B+C and H notified-body EU-type examination certificate).
- **DORA** — national competent authority (NCA) on the Art. 24(1) testing programme + Art. 25(1) 12-way testing modalities portfolio + Art. 26(1) TLPT reports (significant financial entities carry out advanced testing using threat-led penetration testing on a regular basis).
- **AI Act** — provider + market surveillance authority (MSA) + AI Office on the Art. 9(6) testing-for-risk-management outputs + Art. 9(7) real-world-conditions testing per Art. 60 + Art. 15(1) accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity baseline testing throughout AI-system lifecycle.
These are distinct regulatory channels on the testing-evidence object. **The cross-recipient map does NOT generate a notification cascade of D-10.3 itself** — the compliance-testing duty is upstream of any regulatory notification (cross to D-04.3 Regulatory Notification) and feeds the conformity-assessment, MSA inspection, supervisory review, and management-body oversight channels. The D-10.3 testing evidence feeds the D-09.1 Information Security Policies documentation obligation, the D-09.2 Impact & Risk Assessments review cycle (the 5 risk-assessment regimes re-evaluate on testing outputs), and the D-09.4 Records of Processing documentation obligation.

## Scope-overlap principle (operational)

#### Scope-overlap principle (operational)
The high-level SO (`SG-D-10.3.HL`) engages on a given party **if and only if ≥2 applicable regulations have ≥1 Y or Conditional-Y-scope-overlap pair on the same party** (e.g. a DORA significant financial entity that is also a controller deploying CRA-conformant products that are also high-risk AI — a vertically-integrated financial-controller-manufacturer-AI-provider with GDPR + NIS 2 + CRA + DORA + AI Act applicability on the 5-parallel-testing-programme architecture). For a CRA-regulated manufacturer that is also a controller deploying high-risk AI, the GDPR + CRA + AI Act sub-SOs activate on **three different artefacts** (entity-level effectiveness-evaluation vs. product-level conformity-assessment vs. AI-system-level testing for risk management) — the three regimes meet at the controller-manufacturer-AI-provider interface. For a DORA significant financial entity deploying high-risk AI, the DORA + AI Act sub-SOs activate with the DORA-led TLPT (Art. 26(1)) discharging both DORA's advanced-testing obligation and partially covering the AI Act AI-system-testing dimension. **The 5 testing mandates are stacked as 5 parallel testing programmes, not collapsed**: effectiveness-evaluation of security measures (GDPR) + effectiveness-assessment of risk-management measures (NIS 2) + conformity-assessment via OJ-codified modules (CRA) + testing programme with 12-way modalities + TLPT (DORA) + AI-system testing for risk management + real-world-conditions testing (AI Act). **No OJ-level consolidation rule exists** at D-10.3 — the 5 parallel testing programmes are the operative consequence. The high-level SO surfaces the common compliance-testing duty but does **NOT** collapse the per-reg anchor, scope dimension, trigger, threshold, recipient, cadence, or content template, **and does NOT need to resolve any emergent tension** because no OJ conflict exists at D-10.3.
