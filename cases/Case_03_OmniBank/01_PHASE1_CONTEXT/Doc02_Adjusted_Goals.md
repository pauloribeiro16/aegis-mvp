# Case 03 — OmniBank Financial Systems: Adjusted Goals

## 1. Regulatory Applicability (Phase 1 P2)

| Regulation | Applies | Role | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDPR | YES | Controller (& processor for employee data) | Customer PII, financial transactions, credit scores |
| CRA | YES | Manufacturer (Class I — important product) | Mobile app + web platform placing digital products on the EU market |
| NIS 2 | YES | Essential Entity (Annex I — banking) | Banking sector, > 250 employees |
| DORA | YES | Financial Entity (Art. 2(1) credit institution; PSP) | Credit institution and payment service provider |
| AI Act | YES | Provider & Deployer (Annex III §5(b) credit scoring) | OmniScore AI developed in-house and operated for credit access decisions |

## 2. Company Profile Snapshot

OmniBank Financial Systems S.A. — Germany (EU), ECB-supervised; 5,000 employees, ~€1.5B revenue (LARGE scale, critical sector). Products: consumer mobile banking app and web platform; OmniScore AI credit-scoring platform (high-risk, Annex III). Infrastructure: on-premise mainframe core, EU cloud, in-house AI/ML platform. Security organization: ~100 security FTE, 24/7 SOC, certified ISMS, in-house HSM key custody. Sub-domains active: 38/38 (D-07.2 attack-surface limitation added explicitly — Annex I 2(j) and Art. 15(4)-(5) legs).
