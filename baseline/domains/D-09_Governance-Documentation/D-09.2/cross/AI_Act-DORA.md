**Scope comparison:**
- DORA scope: financial-entity three-lines-of-defence + continuous ICT risk identification.
- AI Act scope: AI-system continuous, iterative risk-management system with 4-step process.
- Scope overlap: **Y (when a DORA financial entity deploys high-risk AI).**

**Trigger comparison:**
- DORA trigger: continuous ICT risk identification.
- AI Act trigger: high-risk AI system lifecycle.
- Trigger alignment: **DIFFERENT**.

**Threshold/timeline comparison:**
- DORA: continuous + yearly.
- AI Act: continuous + iterative.
- Tension: **NONE** (no OJ-level consolidation rule for DORA/AI Act — DORA has no equivalent to CRA Art. 13(4) sentence 2).

**Recipient comparison:**
- DORA: NCA.
- AI Act: MSA, AI Office.

**Content template comparison:**
- DORA: ICT risk identification register.
- AI Act: 4-step closed list.

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** COMPLEMENTARY
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **SAME — COMPLEMENTARY**
**Reasoning:** DORA ICT risk framework is entity-level operational; AI Act risk management is AI-system-level continuous. The two stack without OJ-level consolidation (DORA has no equivalent to CRA Art. 13(4) sentence 2).

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **Y (when the DORA financial entity deploys high-risk AI)**. No reclassification required.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation must produce two separate artefacts: DORA Art. 6(4) three-lines-of-defence + AI Act Art. 9 risk-management system. No OJ-level consolidation.
