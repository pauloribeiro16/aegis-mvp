**Scope comparison:**

**Recipient comparison:**

**Classified relationship (from CRDA):** NOT CLASSIFIED (CRDA omitted this pair for D-08.2)
**Verified relationship (after OJ-text analysis):** **SCOPE-DISJOINT** (recommended)
**Reasoning:** The two regimes address entirely different audiences and objects — GDPR workforce training (DPO-catalysed) vs. CRA integrator-facing information (manufacturer-shipped). The Annex II §8(f) integrator audience is a B2B external party, not an internal workforce member. There is no overlap in actors or objects; the substantive classification is scope-disjoint.

**Scope-disjoint test:** Does this pair actually overlap on a single event at a single party? **N** — different audiences (workforce vs. integrator-B2B), different objects, different triggers. Substantively scope-disjoint.

**Downstream implication (Phase 1C Doc 07):** Phase 2 obligation derivation must produce two parallel tracks: GDPR Art. 39(1)(b) workforce role-specific + CRA Annex II §8(f) integrator-facing information. CRDA's omission should be corrected by adding a "scope-disjoint" classification.
