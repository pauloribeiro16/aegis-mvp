# Phase 1 Output — VetCare Connect

> Generated by the aegis-mvp pipeline (Increment 0), following SPEC.md v0.1.0.
> Rules R1–R8 applied; anchors corpus-verified (see RUN_NOTES.md for evidence).

## §1 Company Profile Echo

VetCare Connect, Lda. — a 4-clinic veterinary chain in the Porto area,
Portugal, 15 employees. Operates a web + mobile appointment and reminder app
commissioned from, and hosted by, an external software house (EU cloud SaaS),
plus a practice-management SaaS, card payments via a PSP, and reminder
messaging through the software house. Holds ~12,000 client (pet owner)
records: identity, contact and payment data plus animal clinical history
linked to the owner. Employee payroll runs externally. Staff use individual
logins on the app admin console; the console has no MFA today.

## §2 Applicable Regulations

| Regulation | Verdict | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR | **applies** | Controller processing personal data of ~12,000 data subjects (identity, contact, payment, appointment history) in the EU. |
| CRA | does not apply | Uses commissioned software; does not manufacture or place products with digital elements on the market. |
| NIS2 | does not apply | Veterinary care is not an Annex I/II sector; 15 employees is far below entity thresholds. |
| DORA | does not apply | Not a financial entity. |
| AI_Act | does not apply | Rule-based reminders only; no AI system placed on the market or deployed. |

## §5 Ambiguities & Open Questions

- **AMBIGUITY_REGISTRY card applies:** `GDPR-CP15` (GDPR Art. 32(1), "state of
  the art", VAG/S3) covers AG-D-01.1-001. Canonical resolution followed: the
  four Art. 32(1) measures read as a closed list; proportionate implementation
  for a 15-employee company = encryption at rest + access control able to
  restore confidentiality (pseudonymisation-capable), documented per EDPB
  Guidelines 7/2019 (card refs: C01).
- No registry cards found for Art. 7, 17, 28(3)(b), 29 or 30 as cited here.
- `> ASSUMPTION:` none — every company statement in §1–§3 traces to the input.
- **Open question (to Phase 2):** the software-house contract has not been
  reviewed against the full GDPR Art. 28(3) clause set, including the
  Art. 28(3)(g) end-of-contract deletion/return duty (AG-D-05.3-002 depends on it).
- **Open gap (input-declared):** the admin console has no MFA today —
  AG-D-03.2-001 is a target state, not a statement of current posture.

## §6 Handoff Notes to Phase 2

VetCare Connect is a GDPR-only controller (1 regulation, LOW complexity).
Scope: 4 sub-domains, 6 adjusted objectives (AG-D-01.1-001 … AG-D-09.4-001).
Phase 2 should derive rules/requirements from these objectives, prioritising:
(1) vendor-contract review vs Art. 28(3) — blocking for AG-D-05.3-002;
(2) MFA rollout on the admin console — AG-D-03.2-001;
(3) encryption-at-rest confirmation with the software house — AG-D-01.1-001
(ambiguity card GDPR-CP15 governs the "state of the art" reading).
Retention schedule duties sit outside this subset (D-05.2 candidate for a
future run) — do not silently expand scope.
